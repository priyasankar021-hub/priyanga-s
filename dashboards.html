Case Study · Business Intelligence & Dashboard Design

Dashboards built for real decisions —
across roles, tools, and organisations

A collection of dashboards and analytical views built across Tech Mahindra and Bosch Global Software Technologies — each solving a specific business problem, from daily pipeline visibility to multi-year anonymised performance tracking. Every piece was built in Excel and used in live business reviews.

01 — Most Recent
Sales & Business Leadership Dashboard
Tech Mahindra LimitedExcel → Power BI

A comprehensive weekly leadership dashboard consolidating five data sources — CRM pipeline, leads, billing, revenue actuals, and resource data — into a single interactive view used by 10+ sales and business leaders. Covered pipeline by stage, monthly movement, regional ownership, lead scoring, pipeline aging, revenue by vertical and deal size, billing KPIs, and billed vs unbilled breakdown — all filterable by region and business vertical. Later migrated to Power BI by the organisation.

Design challenge: consolidating five separate data sources with different formats into one coherent model, then designing a layout where each leader could drill into their specific region or vertical without needing a custom report built for them.

A multi-year TCV performance view covering every sales manager across 2021–2024, built in Excel and presented in PowerPoint for leadership. Each column represents an anonymised manager (S# codes) with colour coding by role. Dashed benchmark lines show the expected threshold per role. Green and red dots indicate EAV1 target achievement. Growth % shows movement from previous year achievement to next year target. Yellow circles mark exits, blue circles mark joiners.

Design challenge: showing every manager's performance across four years without revealing names, while simultaneously encoding role, target status, year-on-year growth, and team movement — so leadership could have a meaningful conversation about each person. The anonymisation constraint drove the entire visual design.
